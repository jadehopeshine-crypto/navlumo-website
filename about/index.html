About Navlumo

Our Company

A Shenzhen-based automotive electronics manufacturer helping distributors, wholesalers and brands source reliable products and develop market-ready OEM/ODM solutions.

Our Story

15+ Years in Automotive Electronics

What started with car multimedia systems has grown into a complete product portfolio covering Android head units, portable CarPlay displays, reversing cameras, dash cams, instrument clusters and automotive lighting.

For over 15 years, we have focused on reliable products, practical customization and responsive technical support. Today, we help global distributors and wholesalers build product ranges suited to their vehicles, target prices and local market requirements.
