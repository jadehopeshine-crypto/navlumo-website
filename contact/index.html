Frequently asked questions

Can I order a sample?+

Yes. Sample availability and pricing depend on the product and vehicle application.

Do you offer private labeling?+

Yes. Logo, startup screen, packaging and selected hardware or software changes are available for qualifying orders.

How do I confirm vehicle compatibility?+

Send the vehicle make, model, year, region and a clear dashboard photo. Connector photos are also helpful.

Can you ship internationally?+

Yes. Shipping options are confirmed according to destination, order size and delivery priority.
