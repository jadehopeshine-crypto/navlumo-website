Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions from our global buyers.

Are you a manufacturer or trading company?+

Navlumo is a Shenzhen-based automotive electronics manufacturer supporting product development, production, quality control and international supply.

Can I order samples before bulk production?+

Yes. Samples can be arranged so you can evaluate compatibility, functions and product quality before confirming a bulk order.

What is your MOQ?+

MOQ varies by product model and customization requirements. Tell us the product and target quantity, and our team will confirm the available options.

How do you control product quality?+

Our process includes incoming material inspection, first article inspection, functional and performance testing, aging tests, appearance checks and outgoing quality control.

Do you support OEM & ODM?+

Yes. We support UI and boot logo customization, packaging, product configuration and selected hardware or product development projects.

How do you handle defective products?+

Our technical team reviews the issue and provides troubleshooting or an after-sales solution according to the product, order details and agreed warranty terms.
