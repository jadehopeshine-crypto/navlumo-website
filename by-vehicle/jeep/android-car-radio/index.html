Frequently Asked Questions

Key points to check before sample approval.

Will it fit every Wrangler, Cherokee, Commander or Liberty?+

Fitment depends on model year, trim, dashboard and original radio. Send photos of the dashboard and connectors for confirmation.

Does it retain steering-wheel controls and the factory amplifier?+

Retention depends on vehicle equipment and the selected harness or interface. Confirm each required function in the quotation.

Does the 4G connector mean cellular service is included?+

No. A label on the photographed chassis is not a guarantee of an installed cellular module. Confirm the ordered hardware, bands, antenna and SIM requirements.

Can I connect a reversing camera?+

Provide the camera type, video format and original or aftermarket connection details so the suitable input and adapters can be checked.

What is the MOQ, warranty and lead time?+

Request the current product-specific quotation for sample availability, MOQ, warranty terms and production lead time.
