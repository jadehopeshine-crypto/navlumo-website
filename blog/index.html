AHD vs CVBS Reverse Cameras: What to Confirm

AHD and CVBS describe different analogue video formats. The camera and the receiving display or head unit must support the same format; a matching plug alone does not establish compatibility.

Confirm the supported resolution and video format with the display supplier. Also check supply voltage, connector pinout, cable routing and the physical mounting position.

When replacing a factory camera or combining components from different sources, share the exact model information and test a sample kit before ordering in quantity.

Planning an OEM / ODM Automotive Electronics Project

Begin with your market, product category, vehicle applications and expected quantity. Separate essential requirements from optional features so the proposed solution can be reviewed clearly.

Specify the logo, packaging, user manual, boot logo, UI and hardware changes you need. Availability and development scope depend on the selected product and require confirmation.

Review a sample, record approved specifications and confirm inspection criteria, quantity and delivery arrangements before placing the production order.
